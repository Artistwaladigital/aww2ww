1. Overview & Applicability

This Privacy Policy governs the aww2ww native Android mobile application (Package ID: com.aww2ww.app) and the official website located at https://aww2ww.com, operated by aww2ww Inc. ("we", "us", or "our").

By creating an account, downloading our app, or using any of our creator features, feed services, reels, or messaging features, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information required to deliver a safe, personalized, and high-performance social networking experience:

Data Category Examples Why It Is Collected
Profile & Account Information Full name, chosen username, email address, password hash, avatar/profile image, cover image, biography, category (Creator/Artist/Lifestyle). To set up your account, authenticate you securely, and display your public profile to people you choose to connect with.
User-Generated Content Reels video files, photo posts, stories, captions, comments, and like/reaction interactions. To display your posts in the community feed, enable high-definition reel streaming, and allow followers to discover your creations.
Private Communication Direct 1-on-1 text messages, voice notes, and shared chat media sent between friends. To facilitate private messaging between you and your confirmed friends. Private chats are strictly restricted to the participating users.
Friendships & Social Graph Sent and received friend requests, accepted friendships, follower counts, and community hub memberships. To manage your social circle, send friendship notifications, and show suggested connections in your location.
Device & Log Data Device model, operating system version, browser user agent, IP address, and network request logs. For platform security, preventing bot spam or unauthorized account access, and diagnosing server performance.

Note on Sensitive Data: We do not collect financial payment card numbers, biometric identifiers, or precise real-time GPS tracking data. Account registration only requires a name, username, and email.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Delivering Core Social Features: Allowing you to publish reels, post photos, follow creators, send friend requests, and exchange private messages.
  • Ultra Fast Media Delivery: Transcoding and streaming uploaded video reels and images through our CDN and Cloudflare R2 object storage with minimal latency.
  • Notifications: Alerting you when someone sends you a friend request, accepts your friendship, leaves a comment, or sends you a direct message.
  • Account & Community Safety: Identifying and blocking malicious activity, fake spam accounts, abuse, harassment, or illegal content.
  • Platform Improvements: Monitoring app stability, resolving crash reports, and enhancing user interface responsiveness.

4. Storage & Media Hosting (Cloudflare R2)

aww2ww uses enterprise-grade infrastructure to protect your media and account information:

  • Account & Relational Data: Stored on our dedicated, secured production servers equipped with modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Video & Image Storage: High-definition video reels and user-uploaded media are safely stored in **Cloudflare R2 Object Storage** (Bucket: aww2ww-media) and distributed globally via Cloudflare’s CDN. This ensures fast playback without degrading video quality or risking data leakage.

5. Sharing & Disclosure Policy

We Do Not Sell Your Data: aww2ww does never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or contact details to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

Information is only shared under these limited scenarios:

  • Public Profile & Content: Content you post publicly (such as public reels and posts) is visible to other visitors and creators on the platform.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Trusted cloud infrastructure vendors (e.g., Cloudflare for media CDN, Hostinger for secure server hosting) who operate under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If compelled by lawful court orders, subpoenas, or applicable Indian and international law enforcement requests to protect the rights, property, or safety of aww2ww and our users.

6. Instant Messaging & Friends Privacy

We respect the privacy of your personal conversations:

  • No Mock or Fake Messages: When you open a new profile, your inbox is completely private and empty. No unsolicited promotional messages or fake connections are seeded.
  • Friendship Scoping: Only users who have exchanged and accepted friend requests can start 1-on-1 direct messaging threads.
  • Private Access Control: Conversation threads are accessible exclusively by the two participating accounts. Neither third parties nor other community members can read your private messages.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and global data protection guidelines (including GDPR and Indian DPDP), you have full control over your data:

Right to Deletion: You have the unconditional right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account:

  1. Inside the App: Log in to aww2ww, navigate to Menu ➔ Settings ➔ Delete Account and confirm your request.
  2. Via Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to privacy@aww2ww.com or support@aww2ww.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and your username.
  3. Web Deletion Portal: Visit our dedicated Data Deletion Request Page to initiate immediate data removal.

What Happens Upon Deletion: Within 30 days of your request, your profile, authentication credentials, photos, video reels in Cloudflare R2, comments, friend connections, and chat messages will be permanently deleted and purged from our active systems.

8. Children's Privacy (Under 13)

aww2ww is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 13 years old (or the minimum legal age for social media in your country).

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that an account has been registered by an individual under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that account, its media uploads, and all associated personal records.

9. Security Safeguards

We employ robust organizational and technical security measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3: All communications between your mobile device, browser, and our API servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security certificates.
  • Isolated Cloud Containers: Production application containers operate in isolated Docker environments behind reverse proxies (Traefik) with DDoS mitigation.
  • Access Restrictions: Database access is restricted to authenticated server processes with no open external database ports.

10. Cookies & Local Storage

aww2ww utilizes browser cookies and local storage tokens exclusively to maintain your active login session, remember your selected interface language (English / Bengali / Banglish), and preserve your layout preferences.

We do not use third-party invasive tracking cookies to profile your browsing history on external websites.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you are entitled to several rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You can edit your display name, bio, profile photo, and account details directly in your profile settings.
  • Right to Portability: You can request an export of your uploaded photos and reels.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may log out or request complete account deletion at any time.

User Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Data Safety & GDPR Compliance: How to request permanent deletion of your aww2ww profile, video reels, images, and chat history.

Commitment to User Data Control

At aww2ww (Package ID: com.aww2ww.app), we believe you should always be in full control of your personal data. You have the right to delete your account, your uploaded reels, stories, photos, and messaging logs at any time.

How to Delete Your Account and Associated Data

Method 1 — In-App Instant Deletion

Steps:

  1. Open the aww2ww app on your Android device or visit https://aww2ww.com.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap on Menu ➔ Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm your password and confirm deletion.
Method 2 — Email Support Request

If you are unable to log in or wish our team to purge your data manually, send an email to:

privacy@aww2ww.com or support@aww2ww.com

Subject: Account & Data Deletion Request
Please include: Your registered username and registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted?

Upon receiving and verifying your deletion request, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Profile Data: Name, username, email address, password hash, biography, avatar, and cover photos.
  • Media Uploads: All video reels, posts, and stories hosted on Cloudflare R2 are permanently purged from object storage.
  • Social Graph: Friendships, friend requests, followers, following links, and community memberships.
  • Communications: Private 1-on-1 chats and voice notes.
Retention Timeline: All personal data is purged within 30 days of the deletion request, except where retention is strictly required by applicable financial or legal regulatory obligations.

Questions or Inquiries

For any privacy inquiries, reach us at privacy@aww2ww.com.
